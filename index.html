01 — THE MESSAGE

What is
MOGI.

MOGI is a face someone drew in the back of a notebook and refused to throw away. Big ears, X'd-out studs, a mouth full of braces spelling the name. It exists because we kept drawing it.

✕

Core belief 01

[EDIT] Everything starts as a drawing. If it can't survive being sketched badly at 2am, it doesn't deserve the cotton.

✕

Core belief 02

[EDIT] Keep the garment simple. The logo can shrink to a tag on the hem and the brand still exists.

✕

Core belief 03

[EDIT] Small numbers, no restock. We'd rather twenty people own it than two thousand people scroll past it.

How we get there

  1. STEP 01

    [EDIT] Draw it by hand. Every graphic on every piece starts on paper or an iPad, never a stock file.

  2. STEP 02

    [EDIT] Sample it properly. Real weight, real stitching, real fit — we wear it before anyone else can.

  3. STEP 03

    [EDIT] Release it small. Numbered, finite, gone.

  4. STEP 04

    [EDIT] Build the crew. The brand grows by people, not by ads.

He’s bad at this. He still wakes up every day and makes something, just to be better than yesterday’s MOGI.
